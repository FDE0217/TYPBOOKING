Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

TYP Explore Travel & Tours ("we", "us") operates the website typbooking.netlify.app and the TYP Travel mobile app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send a booking request we collect: your name, date of birth, passport or ID number, email address, phone number, trip details (destination, dates, travelers, tours, transfers) and your preferred payment method.

How we use it

We use this information only to prepare and confirm your trip with airlines, hotels, tour operators and transfer providers, to contact you about your request, to issue your itinerary and to process payment.

Sharing

We share the details needed to make your booking with the travel suppliers involved in your trip. Online payments are processed by Maya; we do not store your card details. Requests are received through Netlify, our hosting provider. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and security

We keep booking records only as long as needed for your trip and legal or accounting requirements. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us at the email below.
